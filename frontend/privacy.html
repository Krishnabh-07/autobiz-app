Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

1. Introduction

AutoBiz AI ("we", "our", "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, isolate, and protect your information when you register a business, book trials, or purchase memberships through our autopilot SaaS platform.

2. Information Collection & Data Scope

We collect essential information to deliver automated services:

  • Business Owner Information: Name, Email, Phone Number, Password Hash, City, and Business Name.
  • Customer / Member Information: Phone Number, Full Name, Email, and Booking Data.
  • Payment details: Transaction values, Mode (UPI, Cash), UTR number, and timestamps.

3. Tenant Isolation & Security (P0 Enforcement)

We utilize an enterprise-grade multi-tenant architecture. Every business account is isolated at the API and database levels. A tenant (Business A) cannot access, read, or alter any information belonging to another tenant (Business B) under any circumstances. Autopilot dashboards are protected behind strict JSON Web Token (JWT) verification.

4. WhatsApp AI Automation & Communications

We send transaction status codes, automated trial reminders, fee alerts, and receipts directly to verified customer WhatsApp numbers. Customers can opt-out at any time by replying with stop commands.

5. Data Deletion and Portability

Business owners can export members, leads, and transaction data directly from their dashboard panel, or request complete account erasure by contacting our support team at support@autobiz.ai.
