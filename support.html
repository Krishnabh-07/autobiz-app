Frequently Asked Questions

How does direct UPI payment work?

When customers choose a plan, the app displays your customized UPI QR code directly on their screen. They pay using standard apps (GPay, PhonePe). You verify the UTR reference number instantly from your dashboard.

Is my data secure from other businesses?

Yes. AutoBiz AI enforces absolute tenant isolation at the API level. Every data request requires a secure JWT verification token. No business can request or access another business's customers or metrics.
